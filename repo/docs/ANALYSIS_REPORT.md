# Analysis report (SYNTHETIC data – pipeline demonstration)

All numbers below come from `results/tables/*.csv` and were produced from simulated data calibrated to the
manuscript's published statistics. They show that the pipeline recovers the *designed* structure; they are not evidence about real materials.

## 1. Design and data
7 formulations × 27 conditions (3 pressures × 3 speeds × 3 setpoints) × 3 batches = **567 runs**; 31-point traces → **17,577 rows**; 189 condition means; 21 batch records; 81-run MR6 companion.
Overall means (synthetic vs paper): COF 0.4512 vs 0.4515; wear 8.03 vs 8.04; CV 6.43 vs 6.43; fade 8.06 vs 8.01; recovery 92.22 vs 92.24; peak T 277.9 vs 278.1 °C; mass loss 0.1451 g (calibrated). See workbook sheet `Paper_vs_Synthetic`.

## 3. Operating conditions
COF is an inverted U (pooled optimum ≈ 9.1 bar, 449 rpm, 238 °C; paper 9.4 / 447 / 225); wear, CV and fade rise almost linearly with P, V, T (wear +10 % over pressure, +12 % over speed, +9.5 % over setpoint). Peak interface T = setpoint + 27–29 °C (99.5 % of variance from setpoint).

## 4. Variance partition (type-II, % of total SS)
COF: formulation 72.0, P 4.1, V 2.1, T 1.9, residual 18.7. Wear: formulation 28.9, P 19.3, V 26.0, T 16.5, residual 8.7. Recovery is least explained (58 % residual). All interaction terms ≤ 1.4 %, so formulation and duty act additively.

## 5. Response surface and batches
Additive quadratic RSM: R² 0.80 (COF), 0.91 (wear); lack of fit not significant (p = 0.36, 0.13); RMSE equals pure-error SD (0.0094 / 0.224 vs 0.0094 / 0.219). Batch ICC ≤ 0.025 for all responses. MR6-only fit: wear R² 0.87 (pred. R² 0.83), COF R² 0.30 (pred. 0.09) – the low COF value reflects a small signal relative to noise, not model misspecification.

## 6. Machine-learning benchmark (held-out R²)
| Scheme | COF best | Wear best |
|---|---|---|
| S1 random | RSM 0.79 | GP/GB/RSM 0.90 |
| S2 leave-one-batch-out | RSM 0.79 | GP/RSM 0.90 |
| S3 unseen speed–T cells | RSM 0.77 | GP/RSM 0.90 |
| S4 unseen formulation | RSM −0.18 (all models ≤ 0) | RSM 0.51, GB 0.47; GP −8.4, ridge −7.5 |

Reproduced conclusions: ML equals but does not beat the transparent factorial RSM for interpolation; no model predicts COF for a new formulation; smooth models (GP, ridge) extrapolate catastrophically for wear while tree models and the operating-only RSM retain R² ≈ 0.4–0.5. Grouped permutation importance (MSE ratio): COF – composition 5.6 vs ≤ 1.2 for P/V/T; wear – composition 3.5, speed 3.4, pressure 2.7, temperature 2.3; CV and fade – speed dominates.

## 7. Multi-objective screening (GP + desirability, 58,653 candidates)
MR6 ranks first (mean D over 27 tested conditions 0.644, min 0.402; MR5 0.625 / 0.372; MR0 0.433 / 0.144). Optimum at the mildest corner (5 bar, 300 rpm, 150 °C): D = 0.890, COF 0.478 ± 0.010, wear 6.19, CV 4.2 %, fade 3.8 %, recovery 95.2 %; the 5 % region spans 5–7 bar, 300–380 rpm, 150–210 °C. Sensitivity: MR6 first in 5/6 scenarios; **MR5 overtakes under the narrow 0.45–0.47 COF band**. Pareto: 2 of 58,653 candidates non-dominated (MR6 and MR5 at the corner).
Caveats: the optimum lies on the design boundary (the objective rewards gentle duty); GP SD (≈0.010 COF, 0.23 wear) is comparable to the MR5–MR6 gap, so that ranking needs confirmation runs; MR6 also differs in BaSO₄ and rubber total (confounded).

## 8. Companion MR6 set (tribofilm)
Coverage correlates positively with COF and recovery and negatively with fade, CV and wear (sheet `Companion_Analysis`); ML ranking changes between hold-out and grouped CV (`T31_companion_benchmark.csv`). These associations are generated by construction and carry no mechanistic evidence.

## 9. Differences from the manuscript
* Values differ slightly because the data are re-simulated (single random draw, seed 20260723).
* The synthetic MR6 COF optimum (9.8 bar, 430 rpm, 233 °C) and some ML scores differ from the paper by sampling noise.
* ML uses fixed hyper-parameters; the paper used nested grouped tuning.
* The paper's Table 24 SVR-COF anomaly (0.133) is not reproduced; targets are standardised inside the SVR pipeline here (0.71).
